Frequently asked questions

Can we collaborate on a video?

Yes, collaboration ideas are welcome. Use the form above to describe your idea, your audience and what you would like to make together.

Do you accept sponsorships?

Sponsorship enquiries are welcome. Please share what you offer and what you have in mind. Any sponsored content should be clearly disclosed to viewers.

Can I request a topic or tool review?

Absolutely. Send the topic or tool name and a sentence on what you want to know. Requests help shape what gets covered next, though not every request can be made into a video.

I found a mistake in a video. What should I do?

Thank you for flagging it. Include the video title and the part that looks wrong, and it will be reviewed.

How long do replies take?

Replies can take a few days. If your message is time-sensitive, mention that in the first line.
