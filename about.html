About LiteAI Lab

A YouTube channel about making AI understandable, useful and a little more fun to work with.

Our mission

LiteAI Lab exists to make AI approachable. We explain how things work, show what is possible with everyday hardware and tools, and keep the focus on practical results over hype.

Every video aims to leave you with something you can try yourself.

What the channel covers

  • Beginner-friendly explanations of AI concepts
  • Running and testing models on your own machine
  • Building small automations and workflows
  • Hands-on looks at AI tools and apps

What you will learn

  • How to get started without special equipment
  • How to judge whether a tool fits your needs
  • How to write clearer prompts and get better results
  • How to spot limits, trade-offs and common pitfalls

Content categories

Videos usually fall into one of four types.

Tutorials

Guided, step-by-step walkthroughs for learning a skill or setting something up.

Experiments

Open-ended tests that explore what is possible, with results shared honestly.

Tools

Overviews of software and workflows that help you get more done with AI.

Reviews

Practical evaluations of AI products, covering strengths, limits and fit.

Milestones

How the lab grows, one step at a time.

  1. The idea

    A simple goal: explain AI in plain language and show it working.

  2. First videos

    Short tutorials and demos that set the tone for the channel.

  3. Growing the library

    More experiments, tool reviews and topics suggested by viewers.

  4. What is next

    Deeper series, better resources and more ways to learn together.
